About Us
Where It All Began
Founded on the principles of minimalism and functional elegance,
Folke was born out of a desire to simplify the wardrobe without
compromising on character.
Our journey started in a small workshop with a single goal: to
create clothing that transcends trends. We focus on a unisex
aesthetic, ensuring that quality design is accessible to everyone
regardless of gender.
Positioned as a mid-range brand, we bridge the gap between fast
fashion and luxury, offering artisanal craftsmanship at an honest
price point.

Fashion That Cares
Eco-Friendly Materials
We prioritize organic cotton, recycled polyester, and
linen. Every fiber is chosen with the planet in mind,
reducing water waste and chemical runoff.
Recycled Packaging
Your order arrives in 100% compostable or recyclable
packaging. We've eliminated single-use plastics from
our entire shipping process.
